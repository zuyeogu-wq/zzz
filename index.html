01c. 7000–5000 BCE

Central Plains · early village life

Peiligang Culture

Peiligang communities are often discussed through the evidence of early villages, pottery and plant cultivation. Look for the quiet clues: storage, tools and everyday vessels tell us how people organised life around food.
